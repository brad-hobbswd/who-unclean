The Promise This book is not written to insult your faith, dismiss Scripture, or demand that every reader arrive at the same conclusion. My purpose is to invite honest examination of the way we interpret God’s Word, the way we treat people, and the assumptions we sometimes defend without question. Throughout these pages, we will examine Scripture, consider the experiences of people who have felt rejected by religious communities, and challenge the responsibility believers carry when speaking in the name of God. You may agree with some passages and wrestle with others. You may find yourself reconsidering something you have believed for years. My hope is that you will read with an open heart, a willingness to listen, and a sincere desire to understand. Above all, I hope this book reminds us that no theological conversation should cause us to forget the humanity of the people involved. The promise of these pages is simple: we will ask difficult questions, examine our hearts, return to Scripture, and remember that God deserves our humility while people deserve our dignity.
