Before We Decide Who Belongs

This book invites readers to return to Scripture with humility and consider how religious labels, assumptions, and traditions influence the way people are treated. Through Peter’s vision in Acts 10, biblical reflection, and contemporary experiences, Bradley Hobbs explores the difference between God’s authority and human judgment.

This is not a call to abandon Scripture. The invitation is to examine our interpretation, listen carefully, and remember the dignity of the people involved in difficult theological conversations.
