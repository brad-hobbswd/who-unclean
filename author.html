About the Author Bradley Hobbs is a writer, educator, minister, and musician whose work explores faith, personal growth, relationships, healing, and the experiences that shape our understanding of God and one another. Through devotional writing, biblical reflection, storytelling, and personal projects, Bradley seeks to encourage readers to examine their faith honestly while discovering the importance of compassion, humility, and grace. His writing often addresses subjects that are difficult to discuss within religious communities, with a desire to encourage meaningful conversations rather than silence, understanding rather than assumption, and love that reaches beyond familiar boundaries. Bradley believes faith should influence not only what we believe, but also how we treat the people around us. His work invites readers to consider the power of words, the importance of listening, and the responsibility believers carry when representing Christ. Who Are You to Call Unclean? When God Has Made It Holy continues that mission by challenging readers to examine the difference between God’s authority and human judgment, while encouraging a deeper understanding of the people religion has sometimes pushed away.
