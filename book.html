About This Book Who Are You to Call Unclean? When God Has Made It Holy is an invitation to examine the language, traditions, assumptions, and judgments that have shaped the way Christians treat others. Beginning with Peter’s vision in Acts 10, Bradley Hobbs explores the challenge of recognizing when human understanding has placed boundaries beyond those established by God. Through biblical reflection and contemporary examples, the book considers religious labels, spiritual gatekeeping, rejection, compassion, and the experiences of LGBTQIA+ individuals who have encountered exclusion within Christian communities. This book does not ask readers to abandon Scripture. Rather, it encourages thoughtful examination of Scripture, humility in interpretation, and a willingness to listen when the experiences of others reveal the consequences of our words and actions. At the heart of the message lies a question that reaches beyond theological debate and into the conscience of every believer: Who are you to call unclean what God has made holy?
