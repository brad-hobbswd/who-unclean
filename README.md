# Who Are You to Call Unclean?

## When God Has Made It Holy

**A book by Bradley Hobbs**

Website: [https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/who-unclean/](https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/who-unclean/)

*Who Are You to Call Unclean? When God Has Made It Holy* explores the tension between religious tradition, human judgment, compassion, and the work of God. Through Scripture and reflection, the book invites readers to examine the assumptions they carry, the labels they place upon others, and the humility required when God challenges our understanding.

The book's central Scripture is Acts 10:15 (KJV):

> What God hath cleansed, that call not thou common.

The passage is considered alongside Peter's encounter and the application found in Acts 10:28, with attention to the context of Scripture and the responsibility to approach people with humility, dignity, and compassion.

## About the Website

This repository contains the website and reading pages for the book. The site provides a place for readers to explore the book's purpose, read the manuscript, learn about the author, and find publication information.

### Website Sections

- **Home:** Introduction to the book and featured artwork.
- **About the Book:** Background and purpose of the project.
- **Contents:** Links to the book's sections and chapters.
- **Dedication:** Dedication page.
- **Promise:** The book's opening promise.
- **Preface:** Introduction to the author's purpose and approach.
- **Chapters 1–10:** The main manuscript.
- **Conclusion:** Closing reflection.
- **About the Author:** Author biography.
- **Publication Information:** Edition and publication details.
- **Copyright:** Copyright and permissions information.
- **Terms of Use:** Website terms.
- **Privacy Policy:** Privacy information for website visitors.

## Reading the Book

Readers can begin at the website homepage and follow the reading links through the manuscript. Chapter navigation is arranged sequentially from the Preface through Chapters 1–10 and the Conclusion.

## Design

The website uses a dark, stone-inspired palette with warm bronze, gold, cream, and restrained crimson accents. Typography combines Cinzel for headings, Lora for reading text, and Montserrat for navigation and interface elements.

The layout is responsive and designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
