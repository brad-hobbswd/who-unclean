Dedication

Dedicated to every person who has ever been told they do not belong, whose faith has been questioned, whose dignity has been overlooked, or whose heart has been wounded by words spoken in the name of God.

May you never forget that your life is more than someone else’s opinion of you.

And to those willing to listen, reconsider, grow, and allow God to challenge what they have always believed, may humility lead you closer to His heart.

May we learn to see people before labels, extend grace before judgment, and never confuse our understanding with the authority of God.
